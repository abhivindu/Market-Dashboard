Latest · 11/18/26Sessions Tue Nov 3 - Wed Nov 18

Yields gave back the flight-to-safety drop and more. Equities: the AI selloff became a broad one. After the Nov 3 midterms, Democratic gains raised the prospect of data-center moratoriums, and on Nov 5 the S&P 500 fell 4.4% (worst day since April 2025), the Nasdaq 5.5% and the Dow 3.4%; Nvidia lost 9.3% and AMD 17.3%. Credit and commodities. HY spreads tightened to 3.20% on Nov 12, then widened back to 3.43% by Nov 17 (FRED), near the 3.44% Oct 30 high; IG is 0.92%. Oil kept falling on U.S.-Iran diplomacy: Brent fell nearly 9% on Nov 4, and on Nov 18 WTI dropped more than 4% to $82.19 and Brent to $90.83 (vs. $96.46 / $104.77 on Nov 2).
